# Evidence Law

## Abstract

This portfolio examines the admissibility and exclusion of confession evidence, with particular focus on the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984, Article 6 ECHR, oppression, reliability and the court's discretion to exclude evidence. The work develops a structured legal argument concerning criminal evidence and procedural fairness.

**Academic context:** LLB (Hons), University of East London.
