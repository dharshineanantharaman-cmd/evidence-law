# Evidence Law

## Assessment

This assessment required the preparation of a prosecution skeleton argument in *R v Ali (Yusuf Ahmed)* concerning the admissibility of a defendant’s confession at trial.

The task required analysis and application of the Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984, Article 6 of the European Convention on Human Rights and relevant case law, including issues of oppression, reliability, access to legal advice and the fairness of admitting the evidence.

## Abstract

This portfolio presents a prosecution skeleton argument addressing the admissibility of confession evidence. It examines the statutory safeguards governing confession evidence and applies relevant authorities to the factual circumstances, including arguments concerning oppression, reliability, legal advice and procedural fairness.

**Academic context:** LLB (Hons), University of East London.
